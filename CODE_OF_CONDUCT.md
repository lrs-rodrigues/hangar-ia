# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Positive behavior includes respect, constructive feedback, focus on the community, and empathy. Harassment, insults, threats, sexualized language, doxxing, and other unprofessional conduct are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through the security contact in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or otherwise moderate contributions and will explain enforcement decisions when appropriate.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
